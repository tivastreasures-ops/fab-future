# Fabulous Future Me — truthful 1–3 minute demo script

## 90-second version

**0:00–0:10 — Landing**  
"Fabulous Future Me is a private creative studio, not a beauty assessment.
Your skin. Your colors. Your rules. Nothing here scores or corrects you."

**0:10–0:25 — Draft**  
"At the drafting table, choose any lipstick color—including a custom hex—and
one finish. Name a look, add its mood and notes, and save it without using
YouCam."

**0:25–0:40 — Save and revisit**  
"Looks are kept locally in this browser. You can reopen and edit a look whether
or not you have rendered it. No sign-in is required."

**0:40–0:55 — Card**  
"A 1080 by 1350 PNG card is composed on this device. Its name and color remain
the artist's choices. Collection title on cards is off by default. Export does
not call YouCam."

**0:55–1:10 — Wardrobe**  
"The wardrobe keeps imagined looks and saved cards together. The palette wall
is a vision board for possible selves—not products to buy."

**1:10–1:25 — Vanity and privacy**  
"Try-on is optional, lipstick-only, and uses a photo you choose. The exact
provider payload is reviewed before submission. We do not retouch your photo.
YouCam's preview may vary from a real product."

**1:25–1:30 — Close**  
"Inform the user. Never overrule the artist."

### Recording notes

- Record the actual flow on an Android phone or desktop browser, rather than
  narrating the screenshot-only montage as if it were a screen recording.
- For an offline-only demonstration, show Draft → Save → Card → Export; no photo
  or provider call is needed.
- Only show a provider result after an explicitly confirmed successful live
  render. Do not call a dry run a result.
- Use only a portrait whose subject knowingly consented to YouCam processing.
- Never expose the server API key or provider authorization header.
