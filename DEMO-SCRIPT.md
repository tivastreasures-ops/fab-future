# Fabulous Future Me — truthful 1–3 minute demo script

## 90-second version

**0:00–0:15 — Enter the studio**  
Show the bright room, the Lookbook books and the three destinations.
"Design the you that you haven't met yet, and keep every version.
Fabulous Future Me is a personal creative playground. Your skin. Your colors.
Your rules. Nothing here scores or corrects you."

**0:15–0:35 — Imagine**  
Name the Lookbook if prompted. Create a named look with a custom hex and finish.
"Imagine starts at the drafting table. Any valid lipstick color is welcome.
Name the feeling, add notes, and save. The cat celebrates, and the look joins
Collect without using YouCam."

**0:35–0:50 — Paint**  
Open Paint and review a dry-run recipe; keep live rendering disabled.
"Paint is lipstick-only. Dry run checks the exact recipe without a photo,
render, or units. An optional live preview requires your chosen photo and two
confirmations. We do not retouch your photo; YouCam's rendering may vary from
the real product."

**0:50–1:10 — Collect**  
Reopen the saved look, then return to the room to show the count, color fan and
warm Paint light.
"Collect keeps every possible self, including looks never rendered. Everything
stays in this browser. No sign-in, shopping, or public feed."

**1:10–1:25 — Card and export**  
Open Card, generate the PNG, download it, then reopen a saved card.
"A readable 1080 by 1350 PNG is composed on this device. The title-on-card
setting is off by default. Cards remain available to reopen and export again;
no YouCam call is needed."

**1:25–1:30 — Close**  
"Inform the user. Never overrule the artist."

### Recording notes

- Record the actual flow on an Android phone or desktop browser, rather than
  narrating the screenshot-only montage as if it were a screen recording.
- For an offline-only demonstration, show Imagine → Save → Collect → Card → Export; no photo
  or provider call is needed.
- Only show a provider result after an explicitly confirmed successful live
  render. Do not call a dry run a result.
- Use only a portrait whose subject knowingly consented to YouCam processing.
- Never expose the server API key or provider authorization header.
